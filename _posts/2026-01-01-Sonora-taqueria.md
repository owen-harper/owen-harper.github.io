## Overview
Sonora Taqueria in Stoke Newington is as authentic as Tacos can get and people know it, so this tiny taqueria is busy and vibey resulting in table space being prime real estate. A simple but delicious menu with modest prices this needs to be high on your list if you’re a taco enthusiast.
