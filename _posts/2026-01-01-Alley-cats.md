## Overview
Alleycats gives side street New York City. A local, family feel serving fantastic New York style pizza and delicious sides to accompany a guaranteed fun meal with a projection backdrop of American sit-coms. In true American fashion, we had to stand in a line for a table but it was worth the wait. It’s an unusual style of pizza for London town, but one that is very welcome here! Other than the slightly slow service, this is a top pick for pizza in London.
