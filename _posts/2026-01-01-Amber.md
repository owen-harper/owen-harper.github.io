## Overview
Heard about this place from an Open Table top 10 list - it lived up to the hype. A spacious modern interior serving unique middle eastern and medetarian dishes full of flavour. The service was particularly good - we asked our server what he would recommend and ended up ordering all of his recomendations and we're so glad we did because it was a special and memorable meal. 

## Whats we enjoyed
Atom, Glazed lamb shoulder
